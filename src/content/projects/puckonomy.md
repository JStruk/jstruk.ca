Puckonomy is a live NHL product. The scoreboard side is games, standings, and the schedule. The other side is the money: the salary cap, contracts, and trades, which usually end up in a spreadsheet.

Search covers players, teams, contract terms, and trades. Cap space, recent signings, and the news around the league sit in the same place as the score. The repository is private. The site is the product.
